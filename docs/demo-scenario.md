# Stewart Demo Scenario

## The Writer's Room

A creative team is developing a new story within a large interconnected cinematic universe. They want to introduce a new character or concept and need to understand how that decision fits within the existing universe.

## Scenario Flow

1. A writer presents a creative proposal to Stewart.
2. Stewart evaluates whether enough information exists to begin the investigation.
3. If context is missing, Stewart asks targeted questions.
4. Stewart selects relevant specialists and delegates independent analysis concurrently.
5. Discovery specialists investigate their assigned domains using Parallel.
6. Stewart passes the combined findings to Impact.
7. Impact analyzes wider consequences, opportunities, audience considerations,
   and tradeoffs.
8. Stewart synthesizes the full investigation into writer-facing guidance.

## Example Proposal

A writer proposes introducing a new cosmic character after a major universe event. The character may become a recurring supporting character connected to existing cosmic organizations.

## Stewart's Investigation

### Lore Agent

Evaluates:
- Existing universe rules
- Related entities
- Established abilities and systems
- Canon compatibility

### Timeline Agent

Evaluates:
- Chronological placement
- Relevant historical events
- Character and organization availability

### Relationship Agent

Evaluates:
- Existing character relationships
- Team dynamics
- Organizational connections

### Impact Agent

Evaluates:
- Narrative weight and consequence scale
- Existing and future story effects
- Audience-facing continuity expectations
- Risks, opportunities, informed options, and tradeoffs

## Final Outcome

Stewart does not approve or reject creative decisions. Stewart provides human creators with analysis, options, and tradeoffs so they can make informed decisions.
